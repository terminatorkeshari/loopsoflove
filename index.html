Our Story

Made with love, since 2023

Loops of Love is an artisan brand, born from a passion for handcrafted perfection and sharing joy. What began with handmade keyrings and scrunchies has blossomed into a beloved accessories brand — with 2,000+ happy customers across India who cherish thoughtful, customizable creations.

Every single loop is woven by hand. From everyday hair accessories to personalized gift hampers tailored for your loved ones' milestones, we bring your vision to life.
